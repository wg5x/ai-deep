# Kernel Upgrade Proposal 001: Autonomous Venture Loop

> Date: 2026-06-07  
> Status: Approved  
> Proposed by: Current AI runtime  
> Approved by: Human supervisor, message: "相信你."

## 1. Target Documents

This proposal affects:

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-07-deep-research-language-kernel-design.md`
- `reports/001-ai-opportunity-intelligence-market-research.md`
- future `mission/`, `methods/`, `protocols/`, `rubrics/`, and `templates/` documents

## 2. Proposed Change

Raise the project objective from:

```text
AI opportunity intelligence plus deep research
```

to:

```text
a self-improving AI commercial loop that discovers, validates, launches,
and learns from real commercial opportunities.
```

Research remains important, but it becomes one stage inside a larger commercial operating loop.

## 3. Reason

The human supervisor clarified that the desired future is not only AI-assisted research or judgment. The deeper goal is a system where AI can handle the commercial landing process itself, with humans acting mainly as observers, supervisors, and approvers.

This reframes the system from:

```text
report generation and opportunity judgment
```

to:

```text
commercial action, validation, revenue feedback, and self-upgrade
```

## 4. Evidence

The current conversation produced these design conclusions:

- The system should not wait for humans to provide research topics.
- It should run a standing mission and autonomously collect AI-related signals.
- Deep research is an output and escalation path, not the root product.
- Commercial reports should be tested by real market feedback.
- Failed commercial experiments should upgrade the research and judgment kernel.
- The desired end state is a self-running AI business loop with human supervision.

The existing market report already supports the first half of this direction: continuous AI opportunity intelligence is more differentiated than generic deep research. This proposal extends that conclusion to include commercial execution and validation.

## 5. Expected Improvement

If accepted, the kernel will evaluate itself by stronger outcomes:

- not only report quality
- not only insight novelty
- not only source quality
- but also user response, conversion, revenue, and validated learning

This should reduce the risk of producing elegant but commercially empty analysis.

## 6. Risks

The upgrade increases ambition and may create scope creep.

Specific risks:

- moving too fast from research into external action
- pretending commercial validation is automated when human trust and distribution still matter
- overbuilding before proving one narrow offer
- creating legal, reputational, or financial risk through unsupervised actions

## 7. Guardrails

The upgraded kernel should require human approval before:

- spending money
- contacting users
- publishing public material
- collecting payments
- making commitments
- changing canonical documents

The system may autonomously prepare materials, proposals, experiments, and analyses.

## 8. Compatibility Impact

This proposal does not invalidate the existing Deep Research Language Kernel. It nests it inside a larger system:

```text
Signal Radar
→ Opportunity Detector
→ Deep Research Kernel
→ Commercial Validation Lab
→ Venture Operation
→ Kernel Upgrade Loop
```

Existing research documents remain useful, but they should no longer be treated as the final product.

## 9. Rollback Path

If this upgrade proves too broad, the project can roll back to the previous scope:

```text
AI Opportunity Radar with Deep Research escalation
```

The new `mission/autonomous-venture-loop.md` can be archived, and future work can focus only on intelligence and research artifacts.

## 10. Recommendation

Approved as the new top-level direction, with one constraint:

```text
Start with one narrow self-validation project:
validate whether AI Opportunity Radar can attract real user interest or payment.
```
