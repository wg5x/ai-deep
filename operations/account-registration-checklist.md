## 5. Certification Guidance

Start without certification.

Reason:

- The first goal is validation, not brand authority.
- Certification may require extra materials and slow down launch.
- Early content should feel like a real experiment, not a finished company.

Consider certification later when:

- users start asking whether this is a business
- there is paid pilot demand
- you need better account trust
- you decide to operate as a company or professional service
