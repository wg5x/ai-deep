## 1. Channel Thesis

Use Xiaohongshu and Bilibili as the first China-facing validation channels.

They should play different roles:

```text
Xiaohongshu = fast signal testing, notes, comments, private-message feedback
Bilibili = long-form trust building, project diary, deeper explanation
```

Do not treat them as identical distribution channels.

Because the human supervisor is in Beijing, the validation loop should be China-first and Beijing-aware:

```text
Chinese content
→ Xiaohongshu/Bilibili discovery
→ WeChat/private follow-up
→ small founder/operator conversations
→ optional Beijing offline coffee chats or small salons
```

The first market is not global SaaS buyers. The first market is Chinese AI builders, operators, consultants, side-project creators, and early-stage founders who can give fast feedback.

## 4. Xiaohongshu Strategy

### 4.1 Job

Xiaohongshu should test:

- which opportunity angles attract attention
- which titles get saves/comments
- whether users ask for the sample brief
- whether users describe their own pain
- whether users want a customized analysis

### 4.2 Content Format

Prioritize:

- carousel notes
- short practical posts
- title-led hooks
- comment prompts
- simple opportunity checklists

Avoid at the beginning:

- heavy technical essays
- long theory posts
- overly abstract agent architecture
- external-link-heavy content
- exaggerated money-making claims

### 4.3 First Content Pillars

Pillar A: AI商机雷达

Examples:

- `这周我看到3个AI机会，不是新闻，是可以验证的方向`
- `别再收藏AI工具了，先看它背后的付费需求`
- `一个AI产品发布后，我会看这5件事`

Pillar B: 从新闻到商机

Examples:

- `Google/Microsoft都在推常驻智能体，普通人能做什么？`
- `为什么“AI帮你搜索”不再是机会，“AI帮你盯事”才是机会？`

Pillar C: 商业实验日志

Examples:

- `我让AI自己验证一个商业项目：第1天`
- `AI机会雷达实验：今天我准备发给10个人`
- `如果没人愿意回复，这个项目就该怎么改？`

Pillar D: 方法论

Examples:

- `判断一个AI商机，我只看这6个维度`
- `AI新闻很多，为什么真正有价值的是“触发动作”？`

### 4.4 First 10 Xiaohongshu Posts

1. `我准备做一个AI机会雷达：不追热点，只找能验证的商机`
2. `这周AI圈最值得创业者看的3个信号`
3. `普通人看AI新闻，高手看背后的付费需求`
4. `AI常驻智能体来了：不是聊天，是帮你盯机会`
5. `一个AI商机值不值得做？我用这张表判断`
6. `别做第101个AI工具导航，机会可能在这里`
7. `我用AI给自己找商业机会：第1次实验记录`
8. `如果你想做AI副业，先问自己这5个问题`
9. `AI机会雷达样例：从一条新闻拆出3个产品方向`
10. `我需要10个真实反馈：这个AI机会简报有用吗？`

### 4.5 Xiaohongshu Call-To-Action

Use soft CTA:

```text
如果你想看样例，可以评论“雷达”。
我会发你一版样例，想听真实反馈。
```

Avoid aggressive CTA:

- guaranteed赚钱
- 夸大收益
- 大量站外导流
- 直接卖课感

## 5. Bilibili Strategy

### 5.1 Job

Bilibili should build trust and explain the full system.

It should test:

- whether long-form viewers understand the thesis
- whether the project diary creates credibility
- whether people want to follow the experiment
- whether viewers ask to join testing or receive briefs

### 5.2 Content Format

Prioritize:

- 8-15 minute videos
- project diary
- screen-recorded research process
- weekly AI opportunity review
- practical frameworks

Avoid at the beginning:

- pure talking-head theory with no artifacts
- too much startup jargon
- pretending the system already succeeded

### 5.3 First Bilibili Video Series

Series title:

```text
我让AI从0开始验证一个能不能赚钱的项目
```

Episode plan:

1. `第1集：我想做的不是AI日报，而是AI商业闭环`
2. `第2集：AI机会雷达是什么？为什么不是普通资讯号？`
3. `第3集：我用AI分析本周3个机会信号`
4. `第4集：我准备把样例发给10个人，看有没有人愿意付费`
5. `第5集：如果没人买，AI到底判断错在哪里？`

### 5.4 Bilibili Call-To-Action

Use:

```text
如果你是AI创业者、顾问、投资观察者，想看样例简报，可以在评论区留言“雷达”。
我会优先找10个人做真实反馈。
```
