# Target Sourcing Plan

> Experiment: 001  
> Run: 004  
> Status: Internal execution plan  
> External contact: Not executed

## 1. Goal

Find 10-20 high-quality targets for the first AI Opportunity Radar outreach test.

## 2. Preferred Target Sources

Use these sources in order:

1. Human supervisor's warm network
2. People who have publicly shared AI building, consulting, investing, or newsletter work
3. Small founder communities where feedback requests are normal
4. AI builder communities
5. Indie hacker communities
6. Product/strategy operators active in AI discussions

## 3. Candidate Discovery Queries

Use these search patterns manually or with approved tools:

```text
"building AI product" founder
"AI consultant" startup
"AI newsletter" founder
"AI agents" indie hacker
"AI tools" product strategist
"AI startup ideas" founder
"AI automation" consultant
"AI devtools" founder
```

## 4. Candidate Record Schema

Record candidates in `outreach-tracker.csv` using:

```text
candidate_id,name_or_handle,persona,channel,why_relevant,qualification_score,status,last_contact_date,response,next_action,notes
```

Do not include private personal data unless the human supervisor provides it or approves it.

## 5. Outreach Batch Rules

Batch 1 should contain:

- 10-20 candidates
- at least 3 personas
- no enterprise procurement-heavy targets
- at least 5 people with visible builder or consulting intent

## 6. Stop Rules

Stop the first batch if:

- 5 consecutive responses say the problem is not real
- 10 contacts produce no meaningful replies
- recipients consistently classify the offer as "just another newsletter"
- a legal/reputation concern appears

## 7. Next Action

Before outreach, prepare the candidate list and let the human supervisor review:

- target relevance
- message fit
- channel appropriateness
- whether the contact is warm, semi-warm, or cold
