# Outreach Script: AI Opportunity Radar

> Experiment: 001  
> Status: Draft  
> External send: Not approved yet

## 1. Short Warm Message

```text
Hey [Name], I'm testing a small AI opportunity radar for builders.

Instead of summarizing AI news, it picks 3-5 signals each week and turns them into product/opportunity hypotheses with suggested validation steps.

Would you be open to reading one sample brief and telling me whether this would be useful for your work?
```

## 2. Founder-Focused Message

```text
Hey [Name], quick question.

I'm testing a weekly AI Opportunity Radar for founders: it watches AI product, open-source, model, and market signals, then turns the strongest ones into concrete product hypotheses and validation steps.

The goal is not more AI news. The goal is to help builders decide what is worth testing.

Could I send you one sample issue and ask for blunt feedback?
```

## 3. Paid Pilot Probe

Use only after a positive response:

```text
If this were customized around your area, would you consider paying for a one-off opportunity brief or a weekly version?

I'm trying to learn whether this is only interesting, or actually valuable enough to pay for.
```

## 4. Feedback Questions

Ask:

1. Is this solving a real problem for you?
2. What part is most useful?
3. What part feels like normal AI newsletter content?
4. Would you want this weekly?
5. Would you pay for a customized version?
6. What would make this a must-have?

## 5. Rejection Capture

If they decline, ask:

```text
Totally fair. Could I ask one thing: is the problem not real for you, or is this just not the right format?
```
