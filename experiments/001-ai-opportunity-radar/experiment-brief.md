# Experiment Brief: Validate AI Opportunity Radar Demand

> Experiment: 001  
> Date: 2026-06-07  
> Status: Ready for human review  
> External action: Not approved yet

## 1. Experiment Question

Will builders, small teams, consultants, or scouts respond to a weekly AI opportunity intelligence service strongly enough to justify continued work?

## 2. Minimum Experiment

Create a sample weekly brief and send a concise offer to 10-20 hand-picked target users after human approval.

Do not build a product first.

## 3. Test Offer

```text
AI Opportunity Radar:
a weekly decision brief that turns fast-moving AI signals into commercial opportunity hypotheses and testable next steps.
```

## 4. Experiment Steps

1. Prepare a sample weekly brief.
2. Prepare the offer message.
3. Identify 10-20 qualified targets.
4. Ask for a reply, call, or paid pilot.
5. Record all replies and objections.
6. Produce a validation memo.
7. Update the kernel based on what failed or worked.

## 5. Success Metrics

Strong signal:

- at least 3 meaningful replies from 20 targets
- at least 2 discovery calls
- at least 1 paid pilot or explicit willingness to pay

Moderate signal:

- 2-3 replies but no payment intent
- users ask for a narrower vertical
- users want the brief but not weekly

Weak signal:

- almost no replies
- praise without action
- users compare it to free newsletters or Perplexity
- users cannot identify a use case

## 6. What We Are Testing

We are not testing whether AI can write a report.

We are testing:

- Is the problem real?
- Is the buyer profile right?
- Is the offer clear?
- Is the output useful enough to trigger action?
- Which use case has the highest urgency?

## 7. Approval Gate

Human approval is required before:

- contacting users
- posting publicly
- collecting payment
- using a real person's name in the target list
