# Offer: AI Opportunity Radar

> Experiment: 001  
> Status: Draft

## Short Offer

AI Opportunity Radar is a weekly intelligence brief for builders and small teams. It watches fast-moving AI signals and turns them into commercial opportunity hypotheses, MVP ideas, and validation steps.

## One-Line Pitch

```text
Stop reading AI news. Start testing AI opportunities.
```

## Longer Pitch

Every week, AI Opportunity Radar identifies 3-5 AI signals that may matter commercially. For each signal, it explains what changed, why it matters, who might pay for a product around it, what gap may exist, and how to test the opportunity quickly.

The goal is not to summarize AI news. The goal is to help builders decide what is worth acting on.

## First Paid Pilot

Offer:

```text
One custom AI opportunity brief for your area of interest.
```

Pilot price options to test:

- Free discovery call plus one paid custom brief
- $49 for one custom opportunity brief
- $99/month for weekly AI opportunity radar
- $300-$500 for a custom research sprint

Do not publish prices externally until the human supervisor approves the test angle.

## Best Buyer Angle

The best early buyer may not be a broad reader. It may be someone already trying to build, invest, consult, or create content in AI.

## Core Promise

```text
I will help you find AI opportunities earlier and test them faster.
```
