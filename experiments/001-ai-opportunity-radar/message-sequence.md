# Message Sequence

> Experiment: 001  
> Run: 004  
> Status: Draft  
> External send: Not executed

## 1. First Message

Use for warm or semi-warm contacts:

```text
Hey [Name], I'm testing a small AI Opportunity Radar for builders.

It is not an AI news digest. Each week it picks a few AI signals and turns them into concrete product or business opportunities, with evidence and suggested validation steps.

I made a sample brief. Would you be open to reading it and telling me whether this would be useful for your work?
```

## 2. Follow-Up After Positive Reply

```text
Thanks. Here's the sample brief:
[link or attachment]

Could you skim it and tell me:

1. Is this solving a real problem for you?
2. Which signal is most useful?
3. Does it feel actionable or just interesting?
4. Would you want a weekly version?
5. Would you pay for a customized brief around your niche?
```

## 3. Paid Pilot Probe

Use only after they show real interest:

```text
I'm testing whether this should become a paid service.

If I created a customized opportunity brief around your niche or product area, would you consider paying for a one-off pilot?
```

## 4. No-Pressure Decline Capture

If they are not interested:

```text
Totally fair. One quick question so I can learn:

Is the problem not real for you, or is this just the wrong format/positioning?
```

## 5. Public Post Draft

Use only after human approval:

```text
I'm testing a small AI Opportunity Radar for builders.

The premise: AI news is not scarce. Commercial interpretation is.

Each week, the radar picks 3-5 AI signals and turns them into:
- why it matters
- who might pay
- what product gap may exist
- how to test the opportunity

I made a sample issue and I'm looking for blunt feedback from AI builders, consultants, and scouts.

Reply if you'd like to see it.
```

## 6. Tone Rules

- Ask for feedback before asking for money.
- Do not claim this is a product yet.
- Do not overstate automation.
- Do not imply confidential access to private sources.
- Make the ask small and easy to decline.
