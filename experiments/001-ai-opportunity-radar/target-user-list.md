# Target User List: AI Opportunity Radar

> Experiment: 001  
> Status: Persona and channel list only  
> External contact: Not approved yet

## 1. Target Personas

### Persona A: Solo AI Founder

Profile:

- building or exploring AI products
- follows AI news but lacks time to synthesize it
- wants product ideas and market timing signals

Potential pain:

- too many ideas
- unsure which signal matters
- wants early opportunity but lacks research time

### Persona B: AI Consultant

Profile:

- helps companies adopt AI
- needs fresh client-relevant ideas
- may need briefs to support client conversations

Potential pain:

- must stay current
- needs industry-specific interpretations
- wants reusable research material

### Persona C: Venture Scout Or Angel Investor

Profile:

- watches emerging AI companies and categories
- cares about weak signals and early markets

Potential pain:

- too many startups and announcements
- wants structured watchlists
- wants early thesis formation

### Persona D: AI Newsletter Or Community Operator

Profile:

- publishes AI content
- needs differentiated angles

Potential pain:

- AI news is commoditized
- needs commercial interpretation and narrative

## 2. Candidate Channels

Use only after human approval:

- personal network
- founder communities
- AI builder Discords
- X/Twitter DMs
- LinkedIn messages
- indie hacker communities
- AI consultant communities
- newsletter operator groups

## 3. Target Selection Rules

Prioritize people who:

- already act on AI information
- can say yes without enterprise procurement
- have visible building, consulting, investing, or publishing activity
- can give a fast reply

Avoid early targets who:

- only consume AI news casually
- have no commercial intent
- require enterprise security review
- are not reachable through warm or semi-warm channels
