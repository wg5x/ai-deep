## 1. Purpose

This rubric decides whether a person or team is worth contacting for the AI Opportunity Radar validation test.

The goal is not to maximize the number of contacts. The goal is to find people who can quickly tell us whether the offer has commercial value.

## 2. Qualification Criteria

Score each candidate from 0 to 2 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI involvement | Casual reader | Uses AI tools | Builds, sells, invests, consults, or publishes in AI |
| Commercial intent | No visible business intent | Exploring ideas | Actively seeking customers, products, deals, or revenue |
| Pain intensity | No clear overload | Sometimes tracks AI manually | Regularly needs AI signal interpretation |
| Ability to decide | Cannot act | Can give feedback | Can book call, pay, refer, or test |
| Reachability | Cold and weak | Public/social reachable | Warm/semi-warm or active in reachable community |
| Feedback quality | Generic opinions likely | Some domain context | Can give specific objections and buying criteria |

Maximum score: 12.

## 3. Contact Priority

Priority A:

```text
10-12 points
```

Use for first outreach batch.

Priority B:

```text
7-9 points
```

Use if not enough Priority A candidates.

Do not contact:

```text
0-6 points
```

## 4. Disqualifiers

Avoid candidates when:

- no commercial or building intent is visible
- they only want general AI news
- they require enterprise procurement
- outreach would be intrusive or inappropriate
- there is no clear reason they would care about AI opportunity discovery

## 5. First Batch Shape

The first batch should be small:

```text
10-20 people maximum
```

Suggested mix:

- 5 solo founders or indie builders
- 3 AI consultants
- 3 newsletter/community operators
- 3 venture scouts or angel investors
- 3 product/strategy operators

The batch should be manually reviewed before external outreach.
