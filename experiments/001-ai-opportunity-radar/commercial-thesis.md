# Commercial Thesis: AI Opportunity Radar

> Experiment: 001  
> Date: 2026-06-07  
> Status: Draft for internal validation  
> External action: Not approved yet

## 1. Thesis

Builders and small teams are overwhelmed by the speed of AI change. They do not need more AI news. They need a standing intelligence loop that turns AI signals into commercial opportunity judgment.

If we provide a high-signal weekly AI opportunity brief with clear evidence, opportunity scoring, and actionable next steps, some target users will be willing to reply, book a conversation, subscribe, or pay for a pilot.

## 2. Target Users

Primary:

- solo founders building AI products
- small startup teams exploring AI product ideas
- AI consultants looking for client opportunities
- venture scouts and angel investors tracking early signals

Secondary:

- newsletter operators
- product strategists
- developer relations teams
- corporate innovation teams

## 3. Pain

Target users face:

- too many AI announcements
- too much duplicated commentary
- difficulty distinguishing hype from useful signals
- difficulty converting information into product ideas
- no persistent memory of past opportunity judgments
- no simple process for validating whether a signal is commercially useful

## 4. Offer Hypothesis

Users may care about an offer framed as:

```text
Every week, get 3-5 AI opportunity signals worth acting on,
with evidence, market interpretation, MVP ideas, and validation steps.
```

The offer is strongest if it is not positioned as news. It should be positioned as decision support for builders.

## 5. Value Proposition

For each signal, the user receives:

- what changed
- why it matters
- who may need this
- what product gap may exist
- how strong the evidence is
- how to test the opportunity this week
- what to watch next

## 6. Validation Criteria

The thesis is considered promising if a cold or warm audience produces at least one of:

- 20%+ reply rate from a small hand-picked outreach set
- 3+ discovery calls from 20 qualified targets
- 5+ waitlist signups from a small landing page test
- 1+ person willing to pay for a pilot report or weekly subscription
- 2+ users asking for a customized opportunity brief

The thesis is considered weak if:

- targets say they already get enough signal from existing tools
- targets treat the offer as a newsletter rather than decision support
- targets like the idea but do not want to act or pay
- the output fails to produce specific next actions

## 7. Key Assumptions

- AI information overload is painful enough to create demand.
- Opportunity interpretation is more valuable than news summarization.
- Small teams will pay for speed and judgment if the signal is specific.
- A manual high-quality service can validate demand before product automation.
- Our document kernel can create better repeatability over time.

## 8. Main Risk

The largest risk is that the product sounds useful but is not urgent. The experiment must test willingness to act, not just verbal interest.
