# Business Retrospective

## Core Rule

Revenue is evidence. Rejection is evidence. Silence is evidence.

Every experiment must upgrade the operating kernel, even when it fails.

## Retrospective Template

```text
Experiment:
Date/window:
Original thesis:
Action taken:
Market response:
Revenue signal:
Non-revenue signal:
What worked:
What failed:
Wrong assumption:
Better explanation:
Next decision: continue / pivot / stop
Kernel upgrade:
Next experiment:
```

## Evidence Levels

| Level | Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Likes only | Attention, not demand |
| 1 | Comments or saves | Weak interest |
| 2 | Private messages or calls | Real curiosity |
| 3 | Trial usage or detailed feedback | Problem may exist |
| 4 | First payment | Commercial proof |
| 5 | Repeat payment or referral | Stronger proof |

## Failure Diagnosis

When an experiment fails, classify the main reason:

- Signal was weak
- Buyer was wrong
- Pain was not urgent
- Offer was unclear
- Price was wrong
- Channel was wrong
- Trust was insufficient
- Delivery was too heavy
- Timing window passed
- Compliance/platform risk blocked action

## Kernel Upgrade Rules

Update the kernel only when there is evidence, not mood.

Good upgrade:

```text
For seasonal events, target merchants at least 10 days before the peak window.
```

Bad upgrade:

```text
World Cup ideas do not work.
```

## Output

End every retrospective with one of:

```text
Continue because...
Pivot because...
Stop because...
```
