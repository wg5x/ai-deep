# Cashflow Offer Builder

## Core Rule

Do not sell AI. Sell the buyer a nearer result.

The first offer should be easy to understand, quick to deliver, and priced low enough to test payment without long trust-building.

## Offer Formula

```text
For [buyer],
I help you [near-term business result]
around [timely trigger or persistent pain],
by delivering [concrete artifact/service]
within [time window],
for [price].
```

## Build Steps

1. Name the buyer with spending power.
2. Name the urgent trigger or pain.
3. Translate the pain into a concrete result.
4. Define deliverables that can be produced with AI leverage.
5. Set a first-test price.
6. Write a simple outreach message.
7. Define what proof or feedback counts.

## Offer Output

```text

Buyer:

```

## First Price Bands

Use these as starting points, then adjust by buyer and trust:

| Offer | Price |
|---|---:|
| Single mini pack | 299-999 RMB |
| 7-day campaign/support | 1,999-3,999 RMB |
| Monthly opportunity/growth support | 3,999-9,999 RMB |

## Guardrails

- Avoid guaranteed revenue.
- Avoid investment advice framing.
- Avoid illegal or platform-risky tactics.
- Keep deliverables concrete: posts, scripts, checklists, campaign plans, customer replies, dashboards, reports.
- Prefer service revenue before product subscriptions.
