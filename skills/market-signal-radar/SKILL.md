# Market Signal Radar

## Core Rule

Do not treat news as information. Treat it as a possible money-flow signal.

AI is the tool, not the subject. The market is the subject.

## Use For

- Daily or weekly signal collection
- Events such as gaokao, World Cup, holidays, exhibitions, policy releases
- China platform trends: Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, WeChat, Douyin
- Beijing local business scenes
- Open-source projects that may reduce execution cost
- Any request asking what is happening now with commercial intent

## Scan Lenses

Always inspect a signal through five lenses:

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Investor | What longer trend or value chain shift is visible? |
| Small merchant | Who can make money this week? |
| Market observer | Where are attention, foot traffic, anxiety, or spending moving? |
| Operator | What can be packaged and sold quickly? |
| AI executor | How can AI cut delivery cost or response time? |

## Point-To-Surface Expansion

Never stop at the first event point.

Expand every important signal into:

```text
point -> surface -> lifecycle -> experiments
```

Example:

```text
Gaokao
-> pre-exam blessing, exam-day services, post-exam relief, score release, application planning, admission, university preparation
-> buyers, sellers, anxieties, spending moments, content materials
-> small cash-flow experiments and larger long-term theses
```

For each surface, ask:

- What happens before this point?
- What happens during it?
- What happens after it?
- Which emotions change across the timeline?
- Which people become buyers, sellers, influencers, or gatekeepers?
- Which needs are short-term, recurring, or long-term?

## Output Format

```text
Signal:
Date/window:
Source/evidence:
Who may spend money:
Who may sell something:
Why now:
Lifecycle surface:
Commercial angle:
AI-assisted execution:
Fast experiment:
Risks/compliance:
Keep watching:
```

## Ranking

Prioritize signals that satisfy at least three:

- Real deadline or seasonal window
- Large affected population
- Strong emotion: anxiety, celebration, fear, pride, urgency
- Local execution path in Beijing or Chinese platforms
- Clear buyer with budget
- AI can reduce cost, speed, or labor
- Can be tested in 1-7 days

## Avoid

- Generic news summaries
- Pure AI industry bias
- Political commentary
- Guaranteed income claims
- Sensitive predictions without public evidence
- Opportunities that need illegal, deceptive, or high-risk actions
